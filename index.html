From PC to pocket

One commander.
Two connected screens.

Command Link reads your game journals on your PC. EDSync brings that information to your phone over your private network.

01 / OPEN COMMAND LINK

Start on your PC.

Open EDSync Command Link on Windows or Ubuntu. Complete the Connection setup with your journal folder and private network, then start the server. Keep your phone or tablet on the same private network.

02 / DISPLAY YOUR CODE

Open Pairing.

In Command Link, go to Pairing. Choose whether the mobile device can view data or also edit plans, then select Generate pairing code. Leave the QR code visible on your PC.

03 / SCAN & CONNECT

Pair your mobile app.

Open EDSync on your phone or tablet and tap PC not connected on Home. Choose Scan pairing QR on iPhone or iPad, or Scan on Android. Allow camera access and point it at the QR code in Command Link to pair.

Already exploring Demo Mode? Turn it off in Settings before pairing your PC.

Each QR code works once and expires after five minutes. Generate a fresh code if it expires or when pairing another device. If the camera is unavailable, use Copy pairing URI in Command Link and paste the full code into the mobile app’s pairing screen.

Leave Command Link running for new journal updates. Previously synced information stays available on your mobile device when your PC is offline.

Before you launch

A few useful answers.

Need a hand? Visit project support ↗

Does my PC need to stay on?

For new journal updates, your PC and Command Link need to be running and reachable from your phone. Previously synced information remains available in the app’s local cache.

How does Command Link get my flight data?

Command Link reads the Elite Dangerous journal files on your PC and shares the resulting information with the devices you pair.

Where does my commander data go?

Command Link stores journal data on your PC and shares it with the phones you pair. EDSync caches data on your phone. Optional online lookups contact their respective services. Read our privacy information for details.

How will Frontier sign-in work?

Optional Frontier sign-in is still being prepared. When available, you’ll sign in through Frontier’s own browser page. You won’t need to register a developer application or supply your own client ID. Local journal pairing is a separate connection.

Can I try the app without connecting my PC?

Yes. Demo Mode is available in every build. Open Settings from the Home screen and enable Demo Mode to explore the bundled sample commander. No PC pairing or Frontier login is required. The screenshots on this site were captured that way.
