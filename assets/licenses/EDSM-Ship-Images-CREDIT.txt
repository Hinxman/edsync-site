EDSync includes adapted copies of in-game ship captures from the EDSM.NET
ED-Ships-ScreenShots collection for the 38 hulls represented by that project.

Source project: EDSM-NET/ED-Ships-ScreenShots
Credit requested by the source project: https://www.edsm.net/

The files were resized, renamed, and normalized to platform-native PNG/WebP
resources. EDSync does not include or execute source code from the project.
